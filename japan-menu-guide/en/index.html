How it works ↓

01 / ON DEVICE

Start with on-device recognition

Apple OCR reads Japanese on your device. A bundled dish glossary helps organize reference translations and prices. On iOS / iPadOS 18 or later, once Apple Translation languages are prepared, remaining Japanese text can be translated into Traditional Chinese. On-device translation currently targets Traditional Chinese only.

02 / PAID CLOUD

Choose Paid Cloud when you need more

A first verified credit top-up unlocks cloud recognition and advanced ordering, and permanently removes banner ads for that device wallet. Cloud results follow the language selected in the app. Photos and credit use are confirmed before submission.

Before your next meal

What does on-device translation need?

Apple Translation requires iOS / iPadOS 18 or later. On first use, it may ask you to download Japanese and Traditional Chinese language resources, then retry. The original Japanese remains available if translation is not ready. Local OCR and glossary recognition do not require a top-up.

Can I rely on every translation and price?

Recognition, translations and prices are for reference. Compare them with the original menu and confirm dishes, prices, ingredients and allergens with the restaurant. Prices with unclear sources are flagged for checking.

Where do my photos and results go?

On-device recognition does not send menu photos to the cloud; local results stay in the current result view. Paid Cloud sends photos and text only after confirmation. Completed cloud results and photos stay in this device’s history until you delete them.

How are purchase benefits kept?

Credits and ad removal are tied to the device wallet record. If payment completes but credit delivery is interrupted, use Recover unfinished purchases instead of buying again. Do not delete the app or wallet data as an initial troubleshooting step.
