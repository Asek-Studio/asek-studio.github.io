GreenTake Golf Privacy Policy

Applies to version 2.0.0

Overview

GreenTake Golf is free to download and ad-free in version 2.0.0. It works offline for golf scorekeeping. Its built-in course directory focuses on Taiwan, and you can manually create courses elsewhere.

Data stored on your device

Rounds, hole scores, equipment, and custom courses are stored only in the app's local SQLite database on your device. This data is not automatically transferred to another device or restored after reinstalling the app.

Some Taiwan course entries may not include hole-by-hole data. GreenTake Golf retains the scorecard snapshot entered for the round, so later directory updates do not change a recorded round.

On iOS, GreenTake uses the camera and microphone when you record a practice video. Director uses the microphone to record narration. Practice videos, club-name snapshots, notes and selected ranges are stored in private app files and excluded from system backups. Choosing Save to Photos adds a copy of the selected clip or edited video to your Photos library. GreenTake does not upload these files. Importing a video from iCloud may require Apple to download it first. Deleting a practice record keeps any copies in Photos.

Course weather and tee-time reminders

Booked tee times are stored on this device. iOS schedules tee-time reminders. When you view a course forecast, the app sends the course coordinates and forecast period to Apple Weather. This feature does not need your device location.

Nearby courses

Only after you choose the Nearby Courses action, GreenTake Golf may request foreground coarse location. It uses the device result only to sort the built-in Taiwan-focused course directory by distance. It does not request background location, measure golf-hole distance, retain a location history, or send location off-device.

Data sharing

GreenTake has no account or remote score synchronization. Scores, equipment, custom courses and the location used for nearby-course sorting stay on your device. This version does not display ads or collect data for advertising.

Practice Pro

Practice Pro is an optional iOS subscription. Apple processes purchases and provides the app with product, subscription and eligibility information through StoreKit. Any trial depends on eligibility reported by Apple. Buying or restoring Practice Pro does not upload practice videos or related data.

The Apple Standard EULA applies to iOS use. This information does not limit rights provided by applicable law.

Earlier versions

Older versions that included ads used Google AdMob to serve non-personalized banner and interstitial ads. Google may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure performance. Those versions use Google UMP for consent where required. They may also fetch public ad settings from GitHub Pages. That request includes no scores, location, account or purchase records. The website host may process IP addresses and standard request logs. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Earlier iOS versions used Apple purchase receipts to recognize paid downloads and purchases of Remove Ads. Version 2.0.0 is ad-free for everyone, including prior purchasers. Apple handles refund requests for Apple purchases and determines eligibility under its rules.

Deletion

You control local app data on your device. Removing the app deletes its local data subject to your device operating system's behavior. Because GreenTake Golf has no account or server copy, there is no cloud record for us to delete.
