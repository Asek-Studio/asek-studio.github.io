GreenTake Golf Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-23

Overview

GreenTake Golf is an offline golf scorekeeping app. Its built-in course directory is focused on Taiwan; courses outside Taiwan can be created manually by the user. Core scorekeeping works offline. The free version includes advertising as described below.

Data stored on your device

Rounds, hole scores, equipment, and custom courses are stored only in the app's local SQLite database on your device. This data is not automatically transferred to another device or restored after reinstalling the app.

Some Taiwan course entries may not include hole-by-hole data. GreenTake Golf retains the scorecard snapshot entered for the round, so later directory updates do not change a recorded round.

Nearby courses

Only after you choose the Nearby Courses action, GreenTake Golf may request foreground coarse location. It uses the device result only to sort the built-in Taiwan-focused course directory by distance. It does not request background location, measure golf-hole distance, retain a location history, or send location off-device.

Data sharing

GreenTake has no account or remote score synchronization. Scores, equipment, custom courses and the location used for nearby-course sorting are not sent to us or to advertising services. The free version uses Google AdMob non-personalized banner and interstitial ads. Google may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure performance. Consent is handled by Google UMP where required; you can reopen Ad privacy choices when available. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we verify store purchase records for permanent ad removal. Original paid iOS buyers remain permanently ad-free. Verification failure keeps ads off. See https://policies.google.com/privacy for Google's policy. The app also downloads a public monetization settings file from our GitHub Pages website to check the free-download switch. This request contains no scores, location, account or purchase records; the hosting provider may process your IP address and standard request logs.

Free download and permanent ad removal

The download price and ad activation are separate settings. A free download does not mean ads are already active. Once enabled, users without ad-free access may see banners on Home, History and Bag, and interstitials at transitions after each nine completed holes, at most twice per eighteen holes. Scoring can continue when an ad is unavailable.

The iOS Remove ads forever product is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase, currently priced at TWD 60 in Taiwan. It is not a subscription and does not renew. The price and currency shown on Apple's purchase confirmation apply. Original paid iOS download customers remain permanently ad-free and do not need to buy again. Ad-free access does not back up or synchronize local golf data.

After reinstalling or changing Apple devices, use the same Apple Account used for the original purchase and select Restore purchases on the Remove ads screen. You may request a refund from Apple; eligibility is determined by Apple under applicable rules. If Apple refunds or revokes the transaction, ad-free access granted by that transaction ends. Separate ad-free access from an original paid download remains protected.

The Apple Standard EULA applies to iOS use. This information does not limit rights provided by applicable law.

Deletion

You control local app data on your device. Removing the app deletes its local data subject to your device operating system's behavior. Because GreenTake Golf has no account or server copy, there is no cloud record for us to delete.
