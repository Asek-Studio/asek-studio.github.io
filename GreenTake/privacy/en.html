GreenTake Golf Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-19

Overview

GreenTake Golf is an offline golf scorekeeping app. Its built-in course directory is focused on Taiwan; courses outside Taiwan can be created manually by the user. The app has no account, remote synchronization, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Rounds, hole scores, equipment, and custom courses are stored only in the app's local SQLite database on your device. This data is not automatically transferred to another device or restored after reinstalling the app.

Some Taiwan course entries may not include hole-by-hole data. GreenTake Golf retains the scorecard snapshot entered for the round, so later directory updates do not change a recorded round.

Nearby courses

Only after you choose the Nearby Courses action, GreenTake Golf may request foreground coarse location. It uses the device result only to sort the built-in Taiwan-focused course directory by distance. It does not request background location, measure golf-hole distance, retain a location history, or send location off-device.

Data sharing

GreenTake Golf does not collect or share your personal data. It does not send local scores, equipment, custom courses, or location to our servers or to third parties.

Deletion

You control local app data on your device. Removing the app deletes its local data subject to your device operating system's behavior. Because GreenTake Golf has no account or server copy, there is no cloud record for us to delete.
